How It Works

From an organization's idea to songs and memories people can keep

Your organization chooses an experience and prepares the setting. Participants join in ways that work for them, human songwriters shape what they hear, and the event becomes a bridge to carefully shared post-event memories.

  1. 1
    Choose an ExperienceChoose the group event or the complete program for your organization.
  2. 2
    Prepare ParticipantsPrepare the group or participant roster without requiring every participant to create an account.
  3. 3
    Stories Become SongsHuman-led story sharing and interviews become a shared song or several participant songs.
  4. 4
    Review & Event PreparationComplete appropriate review while the organization prepares the presentation or concert.
  5. 5
    Presentation or ConcertPresent the shared song or bring participants and families together for the follow-up concert.
  6. 6
    Secure Sharing & KeepsakesReturn to organization materials and privately connect participants and families to what was released for them.
Human from beginning to end: Stories are interpreted and shaped by people who listen for the details, relationships, and meaning that make each participant's experience their own.
