Terms of Service

These terms cover using SongKeep to hear a song, shape one, or plan an experience.

Using SongKeep

You may play the public previews, try a lyric from your notes, and read the plans on Pricing. On Try a song, verse ideas are arranged from the notes you paste. You edit them. They are not a finished recording. Do not share someone else’s private story without their permission.

Plans

Individual plans are personal songs. Choosing one starts a SongKeep sign-in. SongKeep confirms that price before any charge.

Group plans are for an organization or gathering. Plan an experience sends a request. The date is confirmed with you, and the invoice follows that request.

Stories and songs

The stories you share remain yours. SongKeep uses them to create the song you asked for, within the permissions you give. Previews on the site are short SongKeep sketches for listening.

Questions

Write to help@honoralifesong.com, or visit Contact.
