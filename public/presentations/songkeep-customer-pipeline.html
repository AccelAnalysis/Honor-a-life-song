Stage 1 · Awareness

What actually happens

The human and operational work SongKeep performs in this stage.

  1. 1
    Define ideal audiences

    Prioritize organization types, decision roles, geography, use cases, and moments that fit SongKeep.

  2. 2
    Lead with the human result

    Publish reaction moments, songs, stories, short-form video, and emotional proof rather than software features.

  3. 3
    Make the music easy to hear

    Use sample songs and listening content as a primary awareness mechanism.

  4. 4
    Show each organization its use case

    Tailor examples for senior living, businesses, nonprofits, faith communities, healthcare, schools, veterans, and community groups.

  5. 5
    Create multiple paths to discovery

    Use organic and paid media, partnerships, events, PR, direct outreach, and advocate referrals.

  6. 6
    Capture the signal

    Record source, campaign, referral, and engagement so Awareness hands off cleanly to Consideration.

Stage 2 · Consideration

What actually happens

The human and operational work SongKeep performs in this stage.

  1. 1
    Demonstrate fit

    Explain the organizational value proposition and tailor examples to the buyer’s use case.

  2. 2
    Provide credible proof

    Let prospects hear songs, see stories, review outcomes, and learn from testimonials and case studies.

  3. 3
    Compare the offer ladder

    Clarify when the $200, $2,500, and $6,000 experiences are the right fit.

  4. 4
    Qualify explicitly

    Confirm goal, audience, timing, location, budget/funding, authority, and important constraints.

  5. 5
    Run the consultation

    Align decision-makers and stakeholders around scope, participants, timing, and the likely experience.

  6. 6
    Resolve uncertainty

    Answer objections, recommend the best offer, provide a proposal when needed, and nurture buyers who are not ready yet.

Stage 3 · Booking & Purchase

What actually happens

The human and operational work SongKeep performs in this stage.

  1. 1
    Select the experience

    Confirm the $200 group event, $2,500 full experience, or $6,000 Legacy Album.

  2. 2
    Establish the organization

    Create or reuse the organization account and confirm the authorized buyer or signatory.

  3. 3
    Confirm scope and capacity

    Lock location, participant model, deliverables, timing, and SongKeep capacity.

  4. 4
    Execute agreements

    Complete service terms, privacy/media requirements, and album-specific release terms when applicable.

  5. 5
    Complete the commercial step

    Collect payment or deposit, or establish an approved invoice/funding path.

  6. 6
    Create the operating container

    Instantiate the correct Organization Experience, confirm next actions, and launch Stage 4.

Stage 4 · Story Capture

What actually happens

The human and operational work SongKeep performs in this stage.

  1. 1
    Choose the capture model

    Use the right group, participant, family, and album-story structure for the purchased experience.

  2. 2
    Establish participants & contributors

    Identify the people involved, family/representatives, accommodations, and contribution roles.

  3. 3
    Obtain granular permission

    Capture participation, recording, creative use, sharing, performance, photo/video, marketing, and testimonial scopes separately.

  4. 4
    Prepare and schedule

    Offer simple prompts, accessibility support, and interview/story-session scheduling.

  5. 5
    Conduct the human conversation

    Listen, probe, clarify people and relationships, identify themes, and capture the details that make the story true.

  6. 6
    Gather and organize source material

    Collect authorized notes, audio, photos, documents, family input, facts, pronunciations, music preferences, and sensitive-content guidance.

  7. 7
    Close gaps

    Resolve missing facts, unclear relationships, required family input, permissions, and follow-up conversations.

  8. 8
    Review readiness

    Confirm the story is complete enough to hand to a creator without reconstructing the brief from scattered materials.

Stage 5 · Song Creation & Delivery

What actually happens

The human and operational work SongKeep performs in this stage.

  1. 1
    Create the creative brief

    Translate verified source material into a clear emotional idea, narrative arc, tone, musical direction, and boundaries.

  2. 2
    Develop lyrics with version history

    Draft, refine, verify details, and preserve every reviewable version rather than overwriting one document.

  3. 3
    Review, revise, approve

    Collect feedback from the right reviewer, track revision requests, and require authoritative approval before production.

  4. 4
    Produce the music

    Move through composition, arrangement, recording, editing, mixing, and finalization with clear ownership and states.

  5. 5
    Run quality review

    Verify lyric version, names, pronunciations, audio quality, metadata, assets, permissions, and presentation readiness.

  6. 6
    Reveal the experience

    Coordinate the presentation, concert, or release moment — including run of show, accessibility, guests, and permitted media capture.

  7. 7
    Deliver securely

    Provide entitled recipients with the final song, lyrics, approved story/media, album materials, and keepsakes.

  8. 8
    Confirm completion

    Create the authoritative completion event that starts Loyalty and Advocacy timing.

Stage 5 · Song Creation & Delivery

How the SongKeep platform supports it

  • ●

  • ●

    Approved lyrics gate production; tasks move through defined states with owners, deadlines, blockers, and quality review.

  • ●

    Working files, final masters, lyric PDFs, artwork, video, delivery assets, and derivatives are governed media assets.

  • ●

  • ●
    Legacy Album release management

    Track sequencing, AI disclosure where applicable, human creative direction, rights, credits, artwork, metadata, distribution, and live links are managed as one release.

Stage 6 · Loyalty

What actually happens

The human and operational work SongKeep performs in this stage.

  1. 1
    Maintain the relationship

    Keep every past and future experience inside one persistent organization account.

  2. 2
    Make the next step obvious

    Show past/upcoming experiences, a clear Plan Another action, and suggested future dates.

  3. 3
    Orchestrate renewal

    Move completed experience → outcome review → next recommendation → dates → scope → booking.

  4. 4

    Recommend another experience, eligible post-experience products, upgrades, and related organizational opportunities.

  6. 6
    Re-engage at meaningful moments

    Use post-delivery follow-up, anniversaries, seasonal planning cycles, and personalized nurture without overwhelming the customer.

Stage 7 · Advocacy

What actually happens

The human and operational work SongKeep performs in this stage.

  1. 1
    Measure satisfaction

    Ask NPS/CSAT after the experience and learn what was most meaningful or needs recovery.

  2. 2
    Branch by behavior

    Route detractors to recovery, passives to light nurture, and promoters to an advocacy journey.

  3. 3
    Ask for the next advocacy action

    Request a referral, warm introduction, testimonial, public review, or approved share.

  4. 4
    Make advocacy easy

    Provide permissioned story cards, clips, quote graphics, captions, streaming links, and introduction flows.

  5. 5
    Recognize successful advocacy

    Apply tasteful referral rewards or benefits only when the defined success event occurs.

  6. 6
    Amplify authentic proof

    Turn approved testimonials, UGC, reviews, case studies, and releases into Awareness content.

  7. 7
    Attribute the outcome

    Carry referral identity from advocate → inquiry → organization → purchase → revenue.

  • ●
