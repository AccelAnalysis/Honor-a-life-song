Privacy Policy

Your story is yours. This page explains what SongKeep keeps, and what stays with you.

What you share

If you create an account, SongKeep stores the name, email, and organization details you enter. A group booking also stores the experience, date request, and invoice details you submit.

On Try a song, interview notes and the lyric you shape stay in that browser until you create an account. Playing a preview does not require an account, and a preview starts only when you press play. Choosing a personal plan starts a sign-in. That step does not take a payment.

How SongKeep uses it

SongKeep uses what you share to shape a song, run an experience, and follow up on a booking or invoice you requested. Participant and family choices about recording, photos, video, and sharing stay attached to that experience.

Who can see it

A group account is visible to the people that organization invites. A personal song account is for the person who created it. SongKeep does not sell stories, lyrics, or contact details.

Questions

Write to help@honoralifesong.com, or visit Contact.
