Project Ageless

Facility Benefits

Project Ageless is designed so residents participate rather than only watch, while facilities gain a structured community experience with approved outputs and outcome reporting.

What this includes

  • Create meaningful resident engagement around life stories and music.
  • Preserve stories as songs and keepsakes that can be shared with authorized families.
  • Build community goodwill through a structured program and presentation experience.
  • Use approved media and outcome information only within the permissions granted for the program.

Place in the hierarchy

Project Ageless

Facilities gain a participatory program centered on resident stories, family connection, presentation and measurable completion.
